## Ideas to carry forward

The supplied comparisons suggest looking at visual learning, playable graphs,
composition, and adaptive playback as related but distinct capabilities. They
highlight Chromatone and Midinous as contrasting research examples, and propose
using a selected route as a composition while surrounding connections remain
available alternatives.

For theory, these are exploration leads. The claim that no existing product
combines the entire concept remains an unverified hypothesis.
