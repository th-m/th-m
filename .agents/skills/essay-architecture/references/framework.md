# Essay Architecture Framework Catalog

Use this catalog as diagnostic prompts for Michael Dean's three dimensions,
nine elements, and 27 patterns. The descriptions are concise operational
paraphrases, not replacements for the creator's full lessons or rubrics.

## Idea

Idea asks what the essay is made from, what it means, and what promise invites
the reader into it.

### Material

Does the essay select lived and cultural material that can sustain its thesis?

| Pattern | Diagnostic question |
| --- | --- |
| Experience | Do specific biographical moments make the idea concrete and relatable? |
| Argument | Does the essay genuinely test competing positions and earn its conclusion? |
| References | Do external sources create context and conversation rather than decoration? |

### Thesis

Is there a concrete central idea that organizes the material, enters a wider
conversation, and matters beyond the page?

| Pattern | Diagnostic question |
| --- | --- |
| Microcosm | Does a representative example embody the larger thesis at human scale? |
| Response | Is the thesis situated as a meaningful response to an existing conversation? |
| Catalyst | Could the thesis change a reader's action, attention, or outlook? |

### Title

Does the title compress the essay's central promise into language that invites
attention and remains memorable?

| Pattern | Diagnostic question |
| --- | --- |
| Glimpse | Does the title create an immediate, relevant reason to begin reading? |
| Essence | Does it distill the thesis without reducing or misrepresenting it? |
| Melody | Is its sound, cadence, or phrasing satisfying to say and recall? |

## Form

Form asks how the essay controls attention from sentence to paragraph to whole.

### Paragraphs

Does each paragraph stage one coherent movement with an inviting entry, legible
development, and rewarding exit?

| Pattern | Diagnostic question |
| --- | --- |
| Frame | Does the opening orient the reader while creating interest or productive uncertainty? |
| Flow | Do sentence order, transitions, and syntax make the thought easy to follow? |
| Finale | Does the ending deliver consequence, surprise, or momentum into what follows? |

### Structure

Does the arrangement of sections and paragraphs make the essay's logic feel
inevitable without becoming mechanical?

| Pattern | Diagnostic question |
| --- | --- |
| Sequence | Does meaning compound in an effective order? |
| Cohesion | Does every major unit serve the thesis or an intentional counterpoint? |
| Modularity | Are units shaped and bounded so readers can perceive the architecture? |

### Tension

Does the essay create stakes and manage uncertainty strongly enough to sustain
reader investment?

| Pattern | Diagnostic question |
| --- | --- |
| Conflict | Is there a consequential contradiction, obstacle, or unresolved question? |
| Threads | Do multiple tensions develop and reconnect without fragmenting the essay? |
| Hook | Does the opening create a specific question or promise the essay later fulfills? |

## Voice

Voice asks how the writing's implied presence, music, and imagery make the
essay distinct and felt.

### Spirit

Does the prose create a layered relationship among author, subject, and reader?

| Pattern | Diagnostic question |
| --- | --- |
| Tone | Are shifts in attitude or rhetorical mode deliberate and appropriate? |
| Perspective | Do changes in angle, distance, or address add insight rather than instability? |
| Subtext | Does the prose let readers infer meaningful implications without obscuring the claim? |

### Sound

Does the prose use sonic patterning to support meaning, pace, and memorability?

| Pattern | Diagnostic question |
| --- | --- |
| Repetition | Do recurring words or structures deepen the idea rather than merely repeat it? |
| Rhythm | Do sentence length, stress, and punctuation control pace and emphasis? |
| Rhyme | Do echoes of sound add pleasure or force without becoming ornamental noise? |

### Sight

Does the language make abstract thought visible, specific, and symbolically
resonant?

| Pattern | Diagnostic question |
| --- | --- |
| Imagery | Do concrete and figurative images let the reader perceive the meaning? |
| Words | Are individual words precise, economical, and occasionally surprising? |
| Motif | Does a recurring image or symbol accumulate meaning across the essay? |
