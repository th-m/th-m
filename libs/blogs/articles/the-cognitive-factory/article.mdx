# Cognitive Factory

<Lede>
  A product metric crosses a threshold. An agent traces the change to a recent release, edits the code, runs the tests, and deploys the fix. The customer journey recovers before anyone opens a dashboard.
</Lede>

That tempting version hides every important judgment. A metric does not prove
cause, a diagnosis does not authorize action, a test does not prove a customer
effect, and a completed run does not teach the next one.

Removing a person from the interface redistributes judgment across the system
around the model. A cognitive factory makes that system explicit:

<Callout>
  signal → typed observation → hypothesis → bounded decision → agent graph →
  reviewable action → evaluated consequence → retained learning
</Callout>

This loop puts the factory into motion without granting a model unbounded
authority. It is a proposal, not a demonstrated system.

## 1. Sense: Signals Must Become Typed Observations

Suppose PostHog reports a journey drop after a release, Sentry reports more
errors, and runtime telemetry shows pressure. The signals justify
investigation, but identify neither cause nor response.

The factory first translates them into a typed observation that records:

- source, window, provenance, baseline, and confidence;
- the affected journey, service, release, and owner;
- corroborating and contradicting evidence; and
- permitted next steps, expiry conditions, and links to the evidence.

Ontology resolves fields such as `release` and `customer journey` to governing
definitions. The contract makes the claim bounded, traceable, and reviewable,
not automatically true.

## 2. Hypothesize: A Trigger Opens an Investigation

A trigger should open a hypothesis, not declare a diagnosis. At least three
explanations remain live:

1. The release introduced a code regression that broke the journey.
2. The intended change created an unexpected product tradeoff.
3. The timing is coincidental or another event caused the change.

The record keeps alternatives, evidence, confidence, and the next discriminating
observation. **Coherence** asks whether an explanation fits the system.
**Correspondence** asks whether it matches independent evidence.
**Consequence** follows an authorized action that tests the prediction.

<Asset id="trigger-opens-hypotheses-motif" />

Preserving alternatives makes uncertainty available to the policy deciding
what action is justified.

## 3. Decide: Judgment Is Bounded; Authority Stays Explicit

Evidence can narrow the field without deciding what the system may do. The
decision layer chooses among a finite set: investigate, experiment, prepare a
change, request approval, abstain, or escalate.

Hard constraints belong in policy. Bounded ambiguity can use a typed primitive
such as Jev; contested goals and novel exceptions remain with people. The system
still owns thresholds, permissions, escalation, and side effects. A model
proposes a judgment. The system authorizes an action.

For this incident, evidence may authorize investigation and a proposed patch
while withholding deployment. A bounded decision authorizes a kind of work,
not an open-ended agent session.

## 4. Execute: Agent Graphs Make Work Inspectable

Authorized work becomes a directed graph of scoped tasks. Each node names its
inputs, tools, permissions, outputs, checks, dependencies, side effects, owner,
and stop conditions. The graph exposes where evidence entered, where a guess
became a plan, and where an action became possible.

### Graph Context

Graph context links hypotheses, evidence, definitions, owners, systems,
decisions, and outcomes with provenance. The factory can trace which customer
evidence motivated a capability, which definition governs it, and which systems
a change will affect.

Storage is secondary. What matters is tracing evidence and definitions through
a decision to the outcome that may revise them.

### Executable Context

Executable context turns relationships into constraints: a definition becomes
a schema, ownership an escalation path, a promise an evaluation, a failure a
regression case, and a rollback requirement a release gate.

<Asset id="executable-context-card" />

For the incident, nodes reproduce the failure, retrieve context, compare
hypotheses, prepare a patch, run evaluations, and assemble a pull request.
Missing evidence triggers retrieval; unresolved hypotheses trigger escalation.
The graph—not the model—is the durable unit of automation. It preserves where
judgment entered and which authority permitted the next step.

## 5. Evaluate: Close One Signal-to-Outcome Loop

An inspectable graph may produce a verified artifact. The factory must still
compare its result with the predicted consequence:

1. Signals become a typed observation and competing hypotheses.
2. Policy authorizes bounded investigation and a reviewable artifact.
3. Tests, evaluations, policy checks, and the proper authority govern release.
4. Product and operational telemetry confirm, narrow, or reject the hypothesis.

Verification asks whether the artifact passed known checks. Evaluation asks
whether the action produced the intended effect. A passing test can coexist
with a worse customer journey; a recovered funnel can conceal higher operating
cost. The consequence gate therefore names the intended effect, observation
window, acceptable tradeoffs, and authority for interpreting the result.

“Self-driving” should mean a governed cycle, not an alert deploying code
unattended. No cited source demonstrates this complete autonomous path.

## 6. Learn: Retained Consequences, Not Activity, Compound

<Callout>
  Work produces outcomes → outcomes produce evidence → evidence updates context
  and evaluation → better context can improve the next work.
</Callout>

The word **can** carries the argument. The system must retain the hypothesis,
evidence, authority, intended effect, actual outcome, correction, and changed
context or evaluation. Otherwise, more tickets, code, tool calls, or tokens are
only activity.

The incident might produce a regression case, corrected ownership, a revised
release gate, or a failed-hypothesis record. The durable asset compares what the
factory expected with what happened. Reflexion shows that retained feedback can
improve agent trials, not that memory creates organizational learning.

<Asset id="consequence-returns-to-context-motif" />

Measure time to a verified outcome, rework cost, recurrence of known failures,
customer and operational effects, and the decisions teams can make safely.
Lower token cost is not better judgment, and faster generation is not a better
outcome.

### The Cognitive Light Cone as a Workflow Diagnostic

Michael Levin's cognitive light cone becomes a qualitative diagnostic for one
configured workflow:

- **Observe:** What evidence can it sense, with what provenance?
- **Interpret:** Which definitions, relationships, and alternatives can it use?
- **Affect:** What can it change through bounded, reversible actions?
- **Learn:** Which outcomes can revise later context or evaluation?

The score describes the workflow, not the intrinsic rank of a model, agent,
organization, or mind.

<Asset id="light-cone-scorecard" />

The destination is not maximal independent action. It is wider but bounded
capacity, paired with reversibility, escalation, and accountable authority.

## 7. Build the Feedback Chain in Dependency Order

A team can begin with one consequential feedback chain:

1. Choose one workflow and name its outcome.
2. Connect evidence with provenance and ownership.
3. Define the concepts, permissions, and boundaries.
4. Normalize observations, hypotheses, and decision paths.
5. Build evaluation and rollback before authority expands.
6. Represent work and ownership as a graph.
7. Return consequences to context and evaluation.
8. Widen the mandate only when outcomes justify it.

Evidence makes signals inspectable; ontology makes them interpretable; policy
and graphs make action governable; retention changes the next cycle.

The six essays now form one operating system. Vision chooses the direction.
Truth tests the claims. Understanding places judgment where the work happens.
The knowledge factory organizes production, and ontology supplies the map. The
cognitive factory closes the loop.
