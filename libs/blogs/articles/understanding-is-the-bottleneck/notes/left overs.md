# Perceptoin
Looking online or asking AI, you get very optimistic answers about it's novel capabilities. Almost as if it is trying to sell you something...  This undoubdetly doesn't help the fact that many people attribute far more intellegence to these systems and far less artificial than they aught to.

Maybe the AI is trained to talk its self up. Or perhaps the words we use to describe AI are all comingled with competing ideas, are overloaded, and smuggle unwanted baggage. In the worst case we have definitions for a term that intentionally contradict each other.

**_Consiousness_:**

- Neural dynamics of subjectivity defines consciousness as having a **biological component**.
- Computational functionalism explicitly postulates that consciousness can be separated from biological substrate.

I wonder what effect these competing definitions have within the token prediction machines... What if there was a correlation between a given term's semantic definition, the term's recorded usage, and the probability that semantic patterns cohere?
