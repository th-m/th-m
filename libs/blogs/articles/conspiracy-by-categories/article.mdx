# Conspiracy by Categories

<Section index="01" title="What is a conspiracy?">

A conspiracy is a secret agreement to carry out a wrongful scheme.
[Merriam-Webster](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/conspiracy) A conspiracy theory proposes that concealed
coordination explains an event or outcome.

The questions are straightforward: who coordinated, what did they intend,
what did they conceal, and who benefited? Shared interests alone do not
establish an agreement.

</Section>

<Section index="02" title="Theories organized by institutions">

### Government

Secrecy, public authority, research oversight, and accountability.

- **Tuskegee and MKULTRA:** documented research abuses show how institutional
  authority can enable wrongdoing.
- **Hillary Clinton's emails and Epstein's death:** investigations and
  documented failures are distinct from additional cover-up or coordination claims.
- **COVID and fluoride:** questions about communication, policy, commercial
  incentives, and oversight; vaccine definitions, patents, and liability are
  separate claims to assess.
- **WEF / “own nothing and be happy”:** a nongovernmental organization's
  published prediction is distinct from allegations of a plan to implement it.
- **Weather control and chemtrails:** the rocket-test video's reported
  cloud/rain effect does not establish covert atmospheric spraying.
- **Denver Airport, Roswell, the Battle of Los Angeles, and the Moon-landing
  hoax:** claims of hidden facilities, suppressed events, and official deception.
- **Building 7 and broader 9/11 orchestration:** demolition allegations,
  insurance transactions, and prior-knowledge claims require separate evidence.

### Corporate

Protecting revenue, ownership, market position, and research narratives.

- **Monsanto:** patented seeds, licensing restrictions, farmer dependence,
  and claims about product safety and influence.
- **Atrazine / Syngenta:** frog research and allegations of efforts to
  suppress or discredit it.
- **Bovine biotechnology, ticks, Lyme, and alpha-gal:** a proposed connection
  whose mechanism remains unresolved.
- **The Great Reset:** public proposals and allegations of coordinated
  concentration of wealth and power.
- **Suppressed cancer cures:** allegations that commercial interests suppress
  treatments; the particular treatment and suppression still need identification.

### Marketing

Corporate influence on ordinary habits, purchases, and expectations.

- **Kellogg's:** breakfast promotion and claims about children's attention;
  the documented advertising case is separate from the factory-worker story.
- **Dental hygiene:** brushing and mouthwash marketing, medical endorsements,
  and recurring purchases; advertising and product benefits are separate questions.
- **Dietary guidance:** the food pyramid, breakfast promotion, and
  bacon-and-eggs endorsements.
- **Diamond engagement rings:** advertising that turns a purchase into a
  social expectation.
- **Sugar-industry research influence:** funding and framing nutritional
  research to favor commercial interests.

### Secret societies and private networks

Private loyalties, access, and influence over public decisions.

- **Illuminati:** the allegation of a hidden network directing institutions.
- **Skull and Bones and Bohemian Grove:** known organizations or gatherings;
  claims about decisions coordinated there require their own evidence.
- **Presidents as puppets:** allegations of hidden control require identified
  controllers and mechanisms of influence.

### Education system

Who selects accepted knowledge, historical narratives, and curricula?

- **Textbook propaganda:** China's history textbooks and the
  [1989–1990 curriculum changes](https://www.moe.gov.cn/jyb_sjzl/moe_164/201002/t20100220_3446.html) raise
  questions about particular policies, publishers, and historical narratives.
- **Alternative histories:** Atlantis, ancient civilizations, expeditions,
  and contested historical accounts.
- **Göbekli Tepe:** the claim that excavation or conservation decisions
  constrain knowledge; deliberate suppression remains a separate question.
- **Lost technology:** Greek fire, the Antikythera mechanism, the Piri Reis
  map, Tesla, and “Incan airplanes”; surviving artifacts and lost techniques
  are distinct from claims of suppression.

</Section>

<Section index="05" title="Closing remarks">

### Some conspiracies are just guys trying to make money

Recurring sales, restricted competition, favorable research, and protection
from accountability offer financial advantages. Deliberate coordination,
ordinary incentives, opportunism, and institutional failures can produce
similar outcomes. Financial gain and the pursuit of control can reinforce
one another.

### Others seek dominion

The ambition extends from shaping purchases to shaping what people can
know, say, and do. Psychological operations, the Panopticon, surveillance,
anticipated observation, censorship, managed narratives, indoctrination,
and penalties for dissent are methods of control to examine.

When does an institution's authority over conduct become an attempt to
govern conscience and belief?

### Gadianton robbers, the beasts, and institutional capture

[Helaman 6:21–23, 38–39](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/hel/6?lang=eng) describes secret loyalties protecting
wrongdoing and a group's capture of government. [Ether 8:22–25](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/bofm/ether/8?lang=eng)
warns later readers about secret combinations seeking power and gain.

[Revelation 13](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/nt/rev/13?lang=eng) describes a beast from the sea with seven heads
and ten horns, followed by a beast from the earth with two horns like a lamb
and a dragon's voice. The chapter connects deception, compelled worship,
and control over buying and selling. I associate the second beast with the
Antichrist; the chapter calls it another beast.

I read the Gadianton account and the warning about secret combinations as
speaking to our own time. I interpret Revelation's beasts to mean that, in
the last days, all institutions would be captured and controlled by Satan.
That is my theological reading; the historical claims still stand or fall
on their own evidence.
