# Michael Levin on Human Partiality

## Source

[Michael Levin — *Making the Process-of-Life Flourish in Bio and Beyond* (Stewarding the Flame, Episode 1)](https://youtu.be/rcZsGNYjH14?is=xXvqdan1he35PA_O)

## Author observation to revisit

The host appeared surprised that Michael Levin's interest in preserving or advancing the human species could be understood as a specifically human preference—a prejudice toward humans—rather than as a default obligation to promote any other form of life or intelligence equally.

This is a useful note for the post's argument about opportunity: choosing *whose* welfare matters is not a neutral result of optimization. A system can model possible outcomes for humans, animals, biological systems, or artificial agents, but the priority among those outcomes is a normative judgment. Calling a human-centered goal “prejudice” does not make the judgment disappear; it makes the value choice visible and contestable.
