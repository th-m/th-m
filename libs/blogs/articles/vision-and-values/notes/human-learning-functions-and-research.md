# Human Learning: Functions and Research

- **Teaching signals:** Sensory errors, rewards, pain, social feedback, curiosity, goals, and bodily needs guide learning; dopamine carries some reward-prediction errors. [Schultz](https://www.nature.com/articles/nrn.2015.26)

- **Credit assignment:** Neuron-specific dendritic signals may help identify which neural activity contributed to an outcome. [Francioni et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10190-7)

- **Active sampling:** Humans select experiences by looking, acting, questioning, and exploring according to goals and uncertainty. [Gottlieb and Oudeyer](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41583-018-0078-0)

- **Consolidation:** Rapidly acquired experiences are gradually integrated with existing knowledge through replay, interleaving, rest, and sleep. [Complementary learning systems](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7209926/); [Rasch and Born](https://journals.physiology.org/doi/10.1152/physrev.00032.2012)
